Program Description

YNAB Notifier sends daily SMS budget summaries based on your YNAB (You Need A Budget) account data.

Message Frequency

You will receive up to one SMS message per day.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out

To stop receiving messages, reply STOP at any time. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.
