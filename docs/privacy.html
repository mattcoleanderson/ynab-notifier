Privacy Policy

YNAB Notifier — Last updated: March 13, 2026

What We Collect

This application collects your phone number for the sole purpose of sending you daily budget notification summaries via SMS.

How We Use Your Data

Your phone number is used only to deliver SMS notifications that you have opted into. Budget data is fetched from YNAB on demand and is not stored.

Third Parties

SMS messages are sent via Twilio. Your phone number is shared with Twilio solely for message delivery. We do not sell, share, or distribute your information to any other third parties.

Data Retention

Your phone number is stored only as long as you remain opted in to notifications. You may request deletion at any time.
